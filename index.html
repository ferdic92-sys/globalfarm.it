Who We Are

An Italian startup with a global agricultural vision

Global Farm srl is an Italian company born from a simple conviction: world-class agricultural expertise, when channelled into fertile territories with the right institutional backing, can generate extraordinary value for investors and communities alike.

We operate at the intersection of agribusiness, investment facilitation, and international cooperation — bringing Italian know-how to one of Central Asia's most promising agricultural frontiers.

Global Farm's ambition is to become the reference point for foreign investment in Uzbek agriculture — and, over time, across Central Asia. Our goal is to develop a structured pipeline of investment-ready projects, connecting international capital with the region's extraordinary agricultural potential. We believe that sustainable growth requires more than funding: it requires trust, expertise, and long-term institutional relationships — and that is precisely what Global Farm is built to provide.

Historic Partnership

A landmark agreement with the Uzbek Ministry of Agriculture

On March 24, 2026, at the Italy–Central Asia Business Forum in Tashkent, Global Farm srl formalised a strategic cooperation agreement with the Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of Uzbekistan. This agreement marks the beginning of a new chapter in Italian–Uzbek agricultural relations, opening doors to large-scale investment in land, infrastructure, and agri-food production.

What We Do Together

A shared vision for the future of agriculture

01

Building Modern Agricultural Infrastructure

Global Farm brings hands-on expertise in developing agro-industrial facilities — from greenhouses and orchards to processing and distribution units. The Uzbek Ministry of Agriculture identifies the most suitable regions and lands, and facilitates access to permits, certifications, and institutional support. Together, we lay the foundations for a modern, scalable agricultural ecosystem.

02

Knowledge Transfer & Human Capital

One of the pillars of this cooperation is the exchange of expertise. Global Farm introduces Italian best practices in nursery management, variety selection, food safety, quality control, and phytosanitary standards. Training programmes, workshops, and field visits ensure that Uzbek farmers, specialists, and distributors gain lasting, practical knowledge.

03

Attracting Investment & Opening Markets

Global Farm actively works to attract foreign direct investment — with a particular focus on Italian and European partners — and to connect Uzbek agricultural production with international markets. The Ministry provides the institutional framework: state incentives, regulatory support, and coordination with government bodies to ensure a stable and transparent environment for investors.

04

A Long-Term Institutional Partnership

This cooperation goes beyond a single project. With the backing of the Uzbek Ministry of Agriculture and the operational expertise of Global Farm, the agreement creates a long-term channel for joint initiatives, business events, and site visits — a living platform where ideas become ventures and ventures become growth.

Why Uzbekistan

A land of extraordinary agricultural potential

🌍

Strategic Location

Uzbekistan sits at the crossroads of Central Asia, bordering five countries and offering access to a regional market of over 100 million people. Its position along historic trade routes makes it a natural hub for agricultural distribution across the region.

🌱

Fertile Land & Favourable Climate

The country's diverse geography — from fertile river valleys to vast plains — supports a wide range of crops, from fruits and vegetables to grains. The climate is well-suited for greenhouse cultivation and year-round production cycles.

🏛️

Government-Backed Investment Framework

The Uzbek government has made attracting foreign investment a national priority. Through our cooperation agreement with the Ministry of Agriculture, Global Farm investors benefit from direct institutional support, streamlined procedures, and access to state incentives.

📈

An Emerging Market at the Right Moment

Uzbekistan's agricultural sector is undergoing rapid modernisation. Entering now means positioning ahead of the curve — with the advantage of institutional backing, lower entry costs, and a first-mover advantage in a market with significant room for growth.
